# 🎬 GOSSIP STORY — FULL VIDEO GENERATION PROMPTS

## GLOBAL CHARACTER & VISUAL CONSISTENCY

Use the same characters throughout the entire video.

**The Gossip Woman:** unattractive middle-aged Black African woman, expressive face, slightly rough features, suspicious eyes, patterned African dress, colorful headwrap, dramatic facial expressions, naturally nosy personality.

**The Daughter:** beautiful young white woman, long blonde hair, elegant appearance, soft expressive face, usually wearing tasteful light-colored dresses.

**The Young Man:** handsome young Black African man, short black hair, slim-athletic build, simple black or dark clothing, humble appearance, warm smile.

**Rich Father:** wealthy older white man, gray hair, expensive dark suit, stern expression.

**Rich Mother:** elegant middle-aged white woman, blonde hair, sophisticated clothing, concerned expression.

Maintain consistent facial identity, body proportions, clothing style, environment, and cinematic color palette throughout.
